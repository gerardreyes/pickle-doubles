Pickle Doubles

You and a partner against two rivals. Win the rally, hold serve, and stay out of the kitchen when you volley.

  • MOVEDrag the left half like a joystick.
  • HITSwipe the right half (short dink, long drive, down lob) or tap a shot button. Hold a shot button to charge power. Hold the stick sideways to aim.
  • TIPSLow balls can only be dinked or dropped. A star on the court means you can smash. Slice keeps it low. Reach late and it pops up.
  • RULESServe diagonally past the kitchen. Serve and return must bounce. No volleys from the kitchen.
